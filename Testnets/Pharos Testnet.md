## 🇬🇧 English

### ✨ What is Pharos?
Pharos is an **EVM‑compatible Layer‑1 blockchain** focused on **Real World Assets (RWA)**.  
It offers ⚡ high throughput (~0.7s block time, 8k+ TPS), 💵 low fees (1 gwei gas), and 🌍 infrastructure for tokenizing bonds, commodities, and real estate.
